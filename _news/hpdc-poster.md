Two posters by NSDF members — Paula Olaya (UTK), Jakob Luettgau (UTK), Naweiluo Zhou (UTK), Giorgio Scorzelli (UofUtah), Jay Lofstead (Sandia Lab), Valerio Pascucci (UofUtah), and Michela Taufer (UTK) — featuring new NSDF services were accepted by the *2022 ACM International Symposium on High-Performance Parallel and Distributed Computing* ([HPDC](https://www.hpdc.org/)).

The new open-source services are:

- **NSDF-Cloud**: *Enabling Ad-Hoc Compute Clusters Across Academic and Commercial Clouds*
- **NSDF-FUSE**: *A Testbed for Studying Object Storage via FUSE File Systems*

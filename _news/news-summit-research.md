## NSDF Summit 2026 Brings Research Community Together in Chicago to Advance Federated Autonomous Science ##

The National Science Data Fabric (NSDF) brought together researchers, national laboratory scientists, academic leaders, and research infrastructure experts on October 19 for the **NSDF Summit on Federated Autonomous Science**, held in conjunction with PACT 2026 at the Discovery Partners Institute in Chicago.  

The summit provided a forum for exploring how federated data, artificial intelligence, and shared computing infrastructure can accelerate scientific discovery across institutions and disciplines.  

Discussions addressed trustworthy autonomous science, AI-ready research data, reproducibility, interoperability, scientific user facilities, and opportunities for cross-institutional collaboration. Sessions also examined shared infrastructure requirements and priorities for coordination among universities, federal agencies, and national laboratories.  

A central theme was **moving from research challenges and position statements toward actionable collaborative pilots**, with participants considering opportunities to connect scientific expertise, data resources, and computational capabilities.  

The summit highlighted the importance of building a coordinated research community capable of advancing federated autonomous science through shared infrastructure, open collaboration, and interdisciplinary partnerships.

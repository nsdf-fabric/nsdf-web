## TACC Joins Effort To Build AI-Driven National Data Science Fabric ##

Oct 21st, 2026.   
TACC News.  
 

The Texas Advanced Computing Center (TACC) at The University of Texas at Austin has joined the U.S. National Science Foundation (NSF)-funded National Science Data Fabric (NSDF) as a funded partner, helping transform the pilot project into a national-scale cyberinfrastructure and service under NSF’s Integrated Data Systems and Services (IDSS) program.

Principal Investigator Michela Taufer of the University of Tennessee-Knoxville (UTK) is leading a team of experts from UTK, University of Utah, Purdue University, TACC, and MLCommons with additional collaborating institutions from academia, national laboratories, and industry.

“We are living through a scientific renaissance driven by data and AI,” Taufer said. “Modern scientific instruments generate enormous amounts of data every second.”

## Purdue enables AI- and data- cyberinfrastructure, partners in multiple newly awarded NSF projects ##

August 17th, 2026.   
Purdue News.  
 

Purdue’s Rosen Center for Advanced Computing (RCAC) has recently secured funding as a partner on multiple National Science Foundation (NSF) grants. The grants come from the Integrated Data Systems and Services (IDSS) program, and RCAC is a partner on three of the awards in this first cycle, with a total intended award amount exceeding $42 million. With these awards, RCAC will help develop and deploy cyberinfrastructure systems that enable data-intensive and artificial intelligence (AI)-driven research nationwide...

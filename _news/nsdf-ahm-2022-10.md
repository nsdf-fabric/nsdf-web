The second National Science Data Fabric (NSDF) in-person meeting was held on October 11–12, 2022 at the Thomas S. Monson Center at the University of Utah in Salt Lake City, in conjunction with the [IEEE eScience conference](https://www.escience-conference.org/2022/).

**Thomas S. Monson Center, University of Utah** — 411 E S Temple St, Salt Lake City, UT 84111

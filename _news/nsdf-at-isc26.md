The National Science Data Fabric (NSDF) will be featured at the [2nd Workshop on Advancing Autonomous Scientific Discovery at ISC 2026](https://autonomousscience.org/workshops/a2sd-2026/) in Hamburg, where Michela Taufer will join leading experts to discuss how connected instruments, data, AI, workflows, and HPC can accelerate autonomous scientific discovery.

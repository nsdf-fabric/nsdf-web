Michela Taufer (Dongarra Professor at UTK and co-PI on the NSDF project) spoke at the annual Women in Science (WiDS) Worldwide conference on March 8, 2023, at Stanford University — a technical conference featuring outstanding women discussing their exceptional work in data science and related fields in a wide variety of research domains.

She moderated the panel discussion on **Data Democratization**: a powerful means for creating sustainable and equitable communities.
